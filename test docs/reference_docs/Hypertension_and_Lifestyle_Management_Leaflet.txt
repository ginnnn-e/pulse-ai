1. UNDERSTANDING BLOOD PRESSURE READINGS
Blood pressure is recorded as two numbers (mmHg):
- Systolic (top number): Pressure in arteries when your heart beats.
- Diastolic (bottom number): Pressure when your heart rests between beats.
- Normal: Under 120/80 mmHg.
- Stage 1 Hypertension: 130-139 / 80-89 mmHg.
- Stage 2 Hypertension: 140/90 mmHg or higher across repeat visits.

2. MEDICATION ADHERENCE INSTRUCTIONS
- Antihypertensive medications (e.g. Amlodipine, Lisinopril, Losartan) work 
  best when taken at the same time every day (e.g. morning with breakfast).
- Never stop taking your blood pressure medication abruptly, even if you feel 
  well. High blood pressure is often a "silent" condition with no physical symptoms.
- If you miss a dose, take it as soon as you remember unless it is almost time 
  for your next dose. Never take a double dose.

3. DIETARY AND LIFESTYLE TARGETS (DASH DIET)
- Sodium restriction: Limit salt intake to less than 2,000mg daily (< 1 teaspoon).
- Potassium enrichment: Increase intake of fresh leafy vegetables, avocados, 
  bananas, and legumes.
- Exercise: Aim for at least 150 minutes of moderate aerobic exercise (brisk walking, 
  swimming, cycling) weekly.
- Weight management: Even a 5% reduction in body weight significantly lowers blood pressure.

4. HYPERTENSIVE CRISIS — WHEN TO GO TO THE EMERGENCY DEPARTMENT
Check into an emergency room immediately if blood pressure is over 180/120 mmHg 
accompanied by:
- Severe "thunderclap" headache
- Sudden blurred vision or vision loss
- Chest pain, tightness, or shortness of breath
- Numbness or weakness on one side of the face or body
