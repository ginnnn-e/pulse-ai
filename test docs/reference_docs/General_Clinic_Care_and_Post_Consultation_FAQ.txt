PULSEAI CLINIC: GENERAL PATIENT CARE & FAQ GUIDE
Official Outpatient Clinical Practice Guidelines & Post-Consultation Advice

1. How Should I Take My Prescribed Medications?
Always follow the precise dosage and schedule written by your physician on your visit record. Take oral tablets with a full glass of water. Unless specifically instructed otherwise, medications should generally be taken after meals to prevent stomach upset. Never crush, chew, or split extended-release tablets without doctor approval. Never share prescribed medicines with others.

2. What Should I Do If I Miss a Dose?
Take the missed dose as soon as you remember, unless it is almost time for your next scheduled dose. If it is close to your next dose, skip the missed dose and resume your regular schedule. Never double up on pills or take two doses at once to make up for a missed dose.

3. General Medication Safety, Alcohol & Storage
Avoid consuming alcoholic beverages while taking prescription medications, especially antibiotics, anti-inflammatories (NSAIDs), or pain relievers, as alcohol significantly increases the risk of stomach irritation and liver strain. Store all medications in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight, moisture, and out of reach of children. Do not store medicines in bathroom cabinets where humidity is high.

4. Managing Common Side Effects
Mild nausea, drowsiness, or slight stomach discomfort can occasionally occur. Taking tablets with food or a glass of milk usually resolves mild digestive upset. If you feel dizzy, rest seated and avoid driving or operating machinery. If mild side effects persist for more than 48 hours, contact the clinic.

5. Red Flag Symptoms & When to Seek Emergency Care
Seek immediate emergency room care or call emergency services if you experience any of the following severe signs: sudden swelling of the face, lips, tongue, or throat; acute difficulty breathing or wheezing; severe chest pressure or palpitations; a widespread blistering skin rash; high persistent fever (>39°C); or sudden severe weakness or numbness.

6. Follow-up Visits & Medical Certificate (MC) Inquiries
If your doctor scheduled a follow-up appointment, please return to the clinic on the designated date for clinical reassessment. If your symptoms worsen or do not improve after completing your medication course, contact the clinic for an earlier review. For medical leave or digital insurance claim summaries, request an itemized billing receipt from the reception desk.
