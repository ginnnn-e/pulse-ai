================================================================================
AMOXICILLIN TRIHYDRATE (500mg) — ANTIMICROBIAL THERAPY GUIDE
PulseAI Clinical Pharmacology Reference Document
================================================================================

1. THERAPEUTIC INDICATIONS
Amoxicillin is a moderate-spectrum beta-lactam antibiotic used to treat susceptible 
bacterial infections in adults and pediatric patients:
- Upper and lower respiratory tract infections (streptococcal pharyngitis, sinusitis, 
  bronchitis, and community-acquired pneumonia)
- Acute otitis media (middle ear infection)
- Uncomplicated skin and soft tissue infections
- Uncomplicated urinary tract infections (UTIs)
NOTE: Amoxicillin is completely ineffective against viral infections such as colds, 
flu, or COVID-19.

2. DOSAGE AND COURSE COMPLETION
- Standard adult dosage: 500mg orally every 8 hours (three times daily) or 875mg 
  every 12 hours.
- Take with or without food. Swallow capsules whole with a full glass of water.
- CRITICAL INSTRUCTION: You must complete the entire 5-to-7 day prescribed course 
  even if your symptoms improve after 48 hours. Stopping antibiotics early allows 
  surviving bacteria to mutate, leading to infection recurrence and antibiotic resistance.

3. DRUG INTERACTIONS AND PRECAUTIONS
- Penicillin Allergy: Inform your doctor immediately if you have had hives or 
  anaphylaxis to penicillin or ampicillin.
- Oral Contraceptives: Amoxicillin may temporarily reduce the effectiveness of 
  birth control pills. Use an additional non-hormonal barrier method (such as condoms).
- Probiotics: Stagger probiotic supplements at least 2 hours apart from amoxicillin 
  to preserve healthy gut flora.

4. ADVERSE REACTIONS
- Common: Mild diarrhea, nausea, vomiting, loss of appetite.
- Warning Signs: Severe watery diarrhea (>5 times daily), severe abdominal cramps, 
  or widespread skin rash. Contact the clinic immediately if these occur.
